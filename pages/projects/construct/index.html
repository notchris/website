Construct is an art series with "creation / destruction" as the theme. Stylistically, I wanted to experiment with mixing grunge and minimalism by adding small hints of distress either with brushes or procedural noise. The pieces were designed and animated in Blender . Each piece in the series uses modifiers and shader magic to manipulate geometry in interesting ways.

When creating this series, I was inspired by the work of Eduardo Recife and Aeriform.
