In 2022, I wanted to improve my audio production skills so I started learning how to sample / manipulate audio. I was inspired by electronic artists like Nosaj Thing,Baths,Purity Ring,Dntel,Starkey - who have really cool rhythmic or glitchy vocal effects I admired.

I was working on a number of electronic projects at the time, and many of the tracks I made felt empty without vocals. I reached out to a bunch of people on the Twitch Music category, but couldn't find anyone willing to collaborate. Instead, I started making Clips in my friends channels and used those to practice sampling, thus marking the start of the Twitch Clip Song project.

What is a Twitch Clip Song?

A Twitch Clip Song is a...song (usually 1-2 minutes) created using Clips from Twitch. The Clips are sliced up, put into a sampler, and paired with music made by yours truly.

Can I commission a song?

Yes! I just ask for a $10 donation via PayPal or Ko-fi. Commissioned songs can take 2-3 days to complete.

Send me an email with:
- Your twitch username (or the name a streamer you enjoy)
- 3-5 links to clips of your choice
- The kind of style song you'd like.

Can I use a Twitch Clip Song for something?

Yes! You can use the songs made for you however you'd like. If you want to credit me, that's super cool of you!

What do you use to make the songs?

I use Propellerhead Reason as my DAW. I either download clips directly from Twitch or record them from VODs using OBS.
